# KAI SHENG — AI Engineer & Creative Technologist Portfolio

## 1. Portfolio Objectives
- Present Ng Kai Sheng (Universiti Malaya, Bachelor of Computer Science in Artificial Intelligence) as an emerging AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, and Creative Technologist who builds, validates, and deploys real-world intelligent systems.
- Communicate specialization, strongest projects, empirical credibility, and contact paths within 5–10 seconds for recruiters.
- Provide deep architectural, mathematical, and validation transparency for technical interviewers, hackathon judges, and academic reviewers.

## 2. Target Audiences & Journeys
- **Recruiter Journey**: Hero (5s positioning) → Selected Work (3 flagship case studies with headline outcomes) → Credibility & Awards (ISIC-WOU24 Gold, BIICC 2026 Best AI Tech + Gold, UM AI, CGPA 4.00) → Evidence-Mapped Capabilities → `/resume` & `/contact`.
- **Technical Interviewer Journey**: Selected Work → Deep Case Study (`/work/cardioxy-pro`, `/work/foodresilience`, `/work/occupational-skills-demand`) → Interactive Architecture & Validation Simulators → Dataset/Model/Calibration Inspection → GitHub source links.
- **Hackathon Judge Journey**: Problem statement → Hardware/Software innovation → Live interactive simulation of modes/forecasts → Real-world impact & SDG alignment → Awards.
- **Academic / Research Reviewer Journey**: `/about` & Case Studies → Research methodology → Walk-forward & Protocol B/C holdout design → Synthetic ablation & Platt calibration analysis → Explicit limitations.

## 3. Information Architecture & Route Hierarchy
- `/` — Home (Hero, Selected Work, Short Professional Introduction, Evidence-Backed Technical Capabilities, Achievements Ledger, Learning & Engineering Journey, Contact CTA)
- `/work` — Complete Work Index & Engineering Comparison Matrix
- `/work/cardioxy-pro` — Flagship Case Study 01: CardiOxy Pro (AI Healthcare, IoT, Predictive Early Warning, ESP-NOW Offline Resilience)
- `/work/foodresilience` — Flagship Case Study 02: FoodResilience (Multi-Horizon Demand & Price Forecasting, Walk-Forward Validation, Inventory Intelligence, SDG 12)
- `/work/occupational-skills-demand` — Flagship Case Study 03: Occupational Skills & Demand Predictor (Dual TF-IDF + OvR Logistic Regression, Platt Calibration, Protocol B/C & Ablation Analysis)
- `/about` — Engineering Philosophy, Academic Foundation, Research Methodology & Personal Narrative
- `/achievements` — Editorial Honours, Competition Awards & Academic Credentials
- `/contact` — Direct Communication Dock, Social Verification & Availability
- `/resume` — Interactive & Print-Optimized Curriculum Vitae

## 4. Technical Architecture
- **Framework**: Next.js (App Router) with React 19 & TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with 3-layer CSS custom property design tokens (Primitive → Semantic → Component)
- **Animation**: `motion` (`motion/react`) with strict `useReducedMotion` compliance and compositor-first transitions
- **Visualizations**: Purpose-built interactive SVG/Canvas engineering instruments (Hero Computational Telemetry Lattice, CardiOxy Live Vital & Mode Simulator, FoodResilience Walk-Forward Horizon Explorer, Occupational Skills TF-IDF & Calibration Inspector)
- **Content Separation**: Strongly typed structured data modules under `src/data/` (`profile.ts`, `projects.ts`, `achievements.ts`, `capabilities.ts`, `journey.ts`)
