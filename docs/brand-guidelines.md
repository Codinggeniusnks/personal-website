# KAI SHENG — Personal Brand & Visual Identity Guidelines

## 1. Brand Positioning & Essence
- **Name**: KAI SHENG (`Ng Kai Sheng`)
- **Primary Title**: AI Engineer + Creative Technologist
- **Secondary Roles**: Machine Learning Engineer · Artificial Intelligence Undergraduate (Universiti Malaya)
- **Core Statement**: *"I build intelligent systems that turn noisy real-world signals into calibrated predictions, resilient decisions, and measurable human impact."*
- **Positioning**: Not "an undergraduate student learning AI", and never inflated with hollow buzzwords ("world-class visionary", "AI guru"). Instead, Kai Sheng is presented as an **empirical systems builder** whose credibility comes from verifiable architectures, walk-forward validation protocols, Platt-calibrated models, and resilient hardware-software integration.

## 2. Brand Personality & Voice
- **Intelligent & Empirical**: Speaks in concrete metrics (`Macro F1 = 0.7149`, `Brier Score = 0.1668`, `SpO₂ < 90%`, `Walk-Forward Holdout 2024-Q4`), explicit trade-offs, and honest limitations.
- **Ambitious & Disciplined**: Combines academic excellence (CGPA 4.00, Universiti Malaya AI specialization) with international innovation recognition (ISIC-WOU24 Gold, BIICC 2026 Best AI Technology & Gold Award).
- **Precision Field Instrument Aesthetic**: Inspired by Swiss scientific monographs (`ETH Zürich` / `Linear` / `Stripe Press`) and precision signal-bench instrumentation (`Tektronix` / `Dieter Rams`).
- **Anti-Patterns Strictly Refused**:
  - No purple-to-blue neon AI gradients or glowing brains
  - No cyberpunk/gamer tropes or fake bash terminals
  - No meaningless skill percentage bars (`Python 95%`)
  - No nested glassmorphic bloat or generic SaaS templates

## 3. Typography System
- **Display & Editorial Headings**: `Space Grotesk` / `Instrument Serif` (loaded via `next/font/google`) — high structural clarity with editorial gravitas for project titles and monograph theses.
- **Primary UI & Body Copy**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` — crisp, modern, highly legible across long-form case study methodology sections (`65ch–72ch` optimal measure).
- **Technical Telemetry, Code & Tabular Metrics**: `JetBrains Mono` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` — reserved strictly for model hyperparameters (`C = 2.2`, `threshold = 0.42`), evaluation protocols, sensor thresholds, and timestamps.
